# Investment decision

Compare dispatch/appointment slots, +15% yard space and additional staff. [Evidence](../evidence/decisions.json) includes implementation cost, annual opex, time-gain and usability ranges, annual gross/net P10–P90, probability of positive annual net value and conditional payback.

Each option receives 2,000 seeded Monte Carlo draws. Time savings are capped by each record's delay. The hour value and usable fraction vary. Annual net value subtracts recurring opex; year-one cash flow must also subtract implementation cost.

**Pilot dispatch first under these assumptions.** Its conservative net range is stronger. Extra yard space has a negative median annual net value in this scenario; more space is not assumed to create more vehicle volume. Staffing is uncertain after recurring cost.

## Interpreting uncertainty

P10/P90 are percentiles of invented assumptions, not statistical confidence bounds or market forecasts. Inputs are independently drawn; correlated costs, benefits and demand could widen the tails. A favorable probability in this model is not a guarantee.

Payback is the median among draws with positive annual net value. This selection can hide unfavorable cases, so the report shows the positive probability and negative tail. Do not quote payback alone. Validate time usability through an actual pilot and finance-led cost review before committing funds.

## Constraints

No automatic increase in throughput or punctuality is inferred. Capital constraints, lane interactions, safety, yard leases, labor agreements and deployment effort are not empirically modeled. These are decision questions for a real operator, not missing evidence to fill with optimistic constants.
