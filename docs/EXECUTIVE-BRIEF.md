# Management decision memo

**Decision requested:** authorize the design of a dispatch/appointment-slot pilot, not a full rollout or booked savings.

**Problem:** terminal delay has operational and financial consequences, but incomplete registrations, capacity constraints and unrecoverable waiting time can inflate an investment case.

**Recommendation under the invented assumptions:** test dispatch first. Its annual net-value assumption range is stronger than yard expansion or additional staffing in the central case. One-off investment is EUR 35,000 and annual operating cost EUR 45,000. Waiting-time value counts only the usable fraction; annual net excludes the one-off investment.

**What could reverse the recommendation:** lower realizable savings, lower value per hour, delivery failure or costs beyond the assumed range. The sensitivity raster includes zero benefit, break-even fractions and first-year cash. An option with negative value should not proceed merely because it ranks above other negative options.

**Evidence needed before spending:** actual delay variability, lane availability, installation costs, usable resource redeployment, operational adherence and safety/service/workload outcomes.

**Pilot decision rule:** evaluate a pre-agreed minimum worthwhile reduction, interval uncertainty, guardrails and resource redeployment. A statistically detectable improvement alone is insufficient. The existing -7.06 minute result is simulated; it is not a funding justification.

**Data conditions:** reconcile source/fact totals, disclose missing departure coverage and retain quarantine warnings. Assign operational and finance owners for real sign-off.
